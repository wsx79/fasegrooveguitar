**Current release: [v0.3.4](https://github.com/wsx79/fasegrooveguitar/releases/tag/v0.3.4).** The existing layout and all controls are retained; common buttons, menus and colours follow the same family style.

**Fase Groove Guitar** is the guitar of the Fase Groove family, next to
[Fase Groove Piano](https://github.com/wsx79/fasegroovepiano) and
[Fase Groove Drums](https://github.com/wsx79/fasegroovedrums): a VST3/AU instrument that
**writes MIDI guitar parts** in the same 13 house genres. Pick a groove (or roll a new one) and the
chords, shape the feel with swing, humanize and the strum, add fills and variations, listen with the
built-in guide guitar, then **drag it into your DAW as a MIDI clip** and play it with your own
guitar instrument. Common controls share the same style as Piano and Drums, and **LINK** plays all three
together.

## Features

- 🎸 **43 grooves in 13 genres**, the same as Fase Groove Piano and Drums: Piano House, Soulful,
  Deep, Gospel, Jackin', Jackin' Piano, Garage & UK, Afro & Latin, Funky & Disco, '90s Piano House,
  Neo Soul & Broken, Chill & Pads, Dance Stabs. Offbeat chords, the Nile Rodgers chicken scratch,
  disco octaves, highlife picking, two-step chops, swells...
- 🎼 **12 ways to play a chord**, one per row of the grid: down strum, up strum, stab, chank, muted
  scratch, slide, swell, arpeggio, lick, octaves, power chord and root. Each one voiced as a
  guitarist plays it in standard tuning (the full six strings, three high strings, one string...).
- 🎹 **Chords:** the key, **174 progressions** in a browser (as the grooves'): soulful & gospel,
  deep house, jackin' & funky, disco & '90s, garage & UK, afro & latin, neo soul & jazz, circle of
  5ths, modal, chromatic & borrowed (Fase Groove Piano's progressions, and more), built on the
  scale as the piano builds them (triads, 7ths or 9ths); 1 to 8 chords, one per bar (click one to
  change it); the **circle of fifths** of Fase Groove Piano (change key, drag a chord onto another)
  and a new progression round it.
- 🎛️ **MIDI IN:** hold a chord on the MIDI input (your keyboard, or **Fase Groove Piano's MIDI out**
  routed to the guitar's track) and the guitar plays it, with its rhythm, as it changes.
- 🎲 **NEW GROOVE:** a random but musical groove in the genre you choose, from a few chords to
  busy 16th strumming (Density). One die per row, one per part A-D (with locks), TOTAL random.
- 🎚️ **STYLE:** one-click setups by genre (groove, chords, swing, feel, fills, sound) and **RESET**.
- ✏️ **Editable grid:** 12 rows × 32 steps (or 64: four bars). Hit, accent, ghost and **rake** (a slow
  strum); chance and rolls; the muted scratch fills the 16ths between the other hits by itself.
- 🎚️ **VELOCITY** view (with shapes and a die), **MIXER** (volume, mute, push / pull and swing per
  row, ready-made **TIMING** feels), **FILL** view to draw your own fill.
- 🥁 **Fills** every 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars (strum build, octave run, scratch build, chank lift, slide up,
  stab stutter, lick run, arpeggio run, bass walk, swell, drop out, mix, custom), a **big strum**
  after every fill, and a **variation** every 4 or 8 bars.
- 🎬 **Song sections:** Intro, Main, Break, Build-up, Drop, Outro, and the **whole song** as one clip
  with markers (song window).
- 🔊 **Guide guitar:** plucked strings synthesized (no samples) in four sounds (Clean Funk, Warm Jazz,
  Disco Chorus, Nylon) with tone, reverb, volume and the **strum** time. Switch it off and play the
  MIDI with your own guitar.
- 📤 **MIDI:** drag the clip into your DAW (loop, 1–16 bars or the whole song, one track or one
  track per row), save it as `.mid`, or use live MIDI out. **Import a MIDI file**: its rhythm into
  the grid, its chords into the slots.
- 🔉 **Audio export:** the guide sound as WAV: a seamless loop, the whole song, or stems.
- 🧬 **Groove templates** from an audio loop or a MIDI file: their timing and accents on any groove.
- 🔗 **LINK:** the same genre, swing, tempo and play button as Fase Groove Piano and Drums, on the
  same beat.
- 🌍 **Interface in 6 languages:** English, Italiano, Español, Français, Deutsch, Português (**OPTIONS → Language**).
- ⏱️ **Tempo:** synced to the DAW or set by hand; ▶ plays without the DAW (space bar).

Everything is explained in the **[user guide](docs/GUIDE.md)**.

## For developers

### Building from source

You need CMake ≥ 3.22 and a C++17 compiler (Visual Studio 2022 on Windows, Xcode on macOS).
JUCE 8 is downloaded automatically (or use a local copy with `-DJUCE_DIR=/path/to/JUCE`).

```bash
cmake -B build -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=Release
cmake --build build --config Release --parallel
ctest --test-dir build -C Release        # engine tests + plug-in tests
```

The built plug-ins are in `build/FaseGrooveGuitar_artefacts/Release/`.

On Linux you also need: `libasound2-dev libx11-dev libxrandr-dev libxinerama-dev libxcursor-dev
libxext-dev libfreetype-dev libfontconfig1-dev libxcomposite-dev`.

### Publishing a release

1. Add the new version at the top of [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) and
   [CHANGELOG.it.md](CHANGELOG.it.md), and update `VERSION` in `CMakeLists.txt`.
2. On GitHub open **Actions → Build → Run workflow** and enter the version in *release_tag*
   (e.g. `v0.2.0`), or push a tag: `git tag v0.2.0 && git push origin v0.2.0`.

macOS signing and notarization work as in Fase Groove Piano (repository secrets
`MACOS_CERTIFICATE`, `MACOS_CERTIFICATE_PASSWORD`, `MACOS_SIGN_IDENTITY`, `APPLE_ID`,
`APPLE_APP_PASSWORD`, `APPLE_TEAM_ID`); without them the build is signed ad hoc.

### Code layout

```
Source/engine/Guitar.*        the lanes, chords, voicings, progressions and preset grooves by genre (pure C++)
Source/engine/Generators.*    random grooves, variations, song sections, fill styles, arrangement
Source/engine/GrooveEngine.*  the sequencer: chords per bar, strums, swing, humanize, fills -> note events
Source/GuideGuitar.h          the synthesized guide guitar (plucked strings)
Source/PluginProcessor.*      parameters, tempo sync, MIDI in (held chords) / out, clip export, user grid
Source/PluginEditor.*         user interface: grid, chord slots, views, song window, options
Source/Branding.*             logo, name, links
tests/EngineTests.cpp         engine tests (no JUCE dependency)
tests/PluginTests.cpp         plug-in tests: state, grid, chords, MIDI / audio export, transport, MIDI in
```

To add a groove, add a `groove (...)` line to `guitarPatterns()` in `Guitar.cpp`: one line per lane,
each character a 16th note: `X` accent, `x` hit, `g` ghost, `f` rake, `.` rest. A one-bar line in a
two-bar groove plays twice. Add new grooves **at the end** of the list, so saved projects keep theirs.
To add a progression, add it at the end of `progressions()`.
